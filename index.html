Hello!

I'm a GIS Web Developer and Geoinformatics graduate student, specializing in creating interactive mapping solutions that bridge the gap between complex geospatial data and user-friendly web applications. Currently pursuing my Master's at Trier University in Germany, I focus on developing web-based GIS solutions using technologies like Leaflet.js, Python, and PostgreSQL.

My projects range from real-time earthquake monitoring systems to hydrological analysis tools, reflecting my passion for making geospatial data accessible and meaningful. I maintain several open-source projects on GitHub, including a QGIS plugin for water level monitoring and various geovisualization tools.
